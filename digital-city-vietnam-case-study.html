Case 01Product Design · MOCA Platform

A digital travel guide for Quang Ninh*

A B2C iOS app for visitors to Vietnam's Ha Long Bay region. Where to stay, eat, drink, shop and what to see, in one place, designed screen by screen from onboarding to profile.

Client

MOCA Platform

Market

Quang Ninh, Vietnam

My role

Lead Designer, UI/UX

Platform

iOS app, B2C

Deliverables

Flows, hi-fi prototypes, styleguide, components

01Context

One app for everything a visitor needs on the ground.

Quang Ninh is home to Ha Long Bay, one of Vietnam's most visited destinations. The brief was a digital travel guide that brings stays, food, drinks, shopping, attractions and tours together for international travellers, in English.

I owned the UI and interaction design at MOCA Platform: user flows, low and high-fidelity prototypes and the visual system, working with product and engineering in sprint planning.

Design goals

  • Orient fast: a home that answers "what do I need now?"
  • Decide with confidence: ratings, duration, pick-up and reviews up front
  • Earn permissions: ask for location and notifications only after showing value

Scope

  • 13 flows designed in Figma, from onboarding to profile
  • Search, filters, calendar and reviews for every category
  • A shared styleguide and component library behind every screen

02Information architecture

From first launch to a booked tour, in a few taps.

The architecture follows the visitor's real questions. The home is organised by need, and every category shares the same pattern: list, filters, detail, map and reviews. Learn it once, use it everywhere.

Core user flow

Onboarding→Location & notifications→Sign in→Home→ StayEatDrinkShoppingAttractionsTours→ List + filters→Detail→Route map→Reviews · Buy

03Key decisions

Three decisions that shaped the product.

Decision 01

Show value before asking for anything

The first screens sell the experience (hotels, events, restaurants) before any request. Then each permission explains what the user gets in return, with an equal-weight "Later" option.

  1. Three value slides with full-bleed local photography
  2. Location request framed as "nearby hotels, restaurants and tours"
  3. Sign-in comes last, with email, Facebook or Google

Decision 02

A home organised by need, not by content type

Six large entry points (Stay, Eat, Drink, Shopping, Attractions, Tours) sit over a hero of the bay, with the region switcher and live weather always at the top. Recommendations follow below.

  1. Region selector and current weather in the header
  2. Six tappable categories as a 3×2 grid
  3. Personalised recommendations with rating and review count

Decision 03

Tours you can understand before you buy

Tour cards show rating, duration, pick-up and distance at a glance. The detail screen adds what's included, a numbered route map and traveller reviews, so the decision happens inside the app.

  1. Quick filters for day trips, walking and boat tours
  2. Route maps with numbered stops and directions
  3. Filters by duration, category and traveller rating

04Screens

The full journey, screen by screen.

05Design system

One styleguide, every screen.

Every screen is built from a shared styleguide and a component library in Figma: colour tokens, a Montserrat type scale, buttons, cards and form styles. It keeps 13 flows consistent and gives engineering a single reference.
