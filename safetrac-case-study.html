Case 02Product Design · MOCA Platform

Contact tracing with SafeTrac*

A COVID-19 exposure notification app for Puerto Rico, rolled out across all of its schools. Designed to feel safe at a moment of fear and mistrust.

Client

MOCA Platform

Market

Puerto Rico

My role

Lead Designer, UI/UX

Platform

iOS app, public health

Outcome

Rolled out across all schools

01Context

Asking people to install a tracing app in the middle of a pandemic.

Contact tracing only works if enough people install it, and people only install it if they trust it. The design had to earn trust before asking for permissions, explain privacy in plain Spanish and make personal risk readable at a glance.

At MOCA Platform I led the UI and interaction design: onboarding, home, risk states and the positive-test flow, with prototypes and sprint planning alongside product and engineering. I also led the UI of Cuidémonos, a COVID-19 app for Colombia.

Design goals

  • Trust first: say what the app does not collect, before anything else
  • Glanceable risk: one word and one colour tell you where you stand
  • A clear next step for every risk level

Constraints

  • Rolled out in schools, so it had to work for students and adults alike
  • Bluetooth Low Energy exposure notifications, no GPS
  • Health guidance that must be unambiguous

02Information architecture

A short path from install to protected.

Core user flow

Splash→We care about you→Privacy & consent→Exposure notifications→App notifications→Resident or tourist→Home · risk state→ Risk guidanceGeneral infoReport positive test

03Key decisions

Three decisions that built trust.

Decision 01

Say what the app does not collect

Before any permission, the privacy screen states plainly that SafeTrac does not collect name, date of birth, address, phone, email or GPS location, and cannot track identity. Consent is explicit, with checkboxes.

  1. Privacy explained in short, plain sentences
  2. Explicit consent: age and privacy policy checkboxes
  3. Bluetooth exposure explained before activation

Decision 02

Risk you can read in one second

The home header changes with the user's state: "Tu estado de riesgo es bajo / medio / alto". Contact tracing shows as a clear ACTIVO or INACTIVO toggle, so users always know whether they are protected.

  1. One sentence, one emphasised word for the risk level
  2. Tracing state as a large label plus toggle
  3. Two secondary actions: general info and report a test

Decision 03

Every risk level comes with an action

The risk guide pairs each level with what to do next, using a traffic-light palette: keep safety measures, or stay home and inform the health authorities. Reporting a positive test takes one verification code.

  1. Green, orange and red cards with a single instruction each
  2. Plain-language guidance, no medical jargon
  3. Positive test report in one step with an operator code

05Outcome

From prototype to every school in Puerto Rico.

SafeTrac was implemented across all schools in Puerto Rico. In parallel I led the UI of Cuidémonos, a COVID-19 app for Colombia.
