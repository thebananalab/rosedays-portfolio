The Challenge

An events app with no clear narrative or differentiation from the competition. Build a brand with its own character, emotionally recognizable and ready to connect.

My Role

Full brand and communication management. Visual and editorial oversight, tone-of-voice definition, content direction, and rollout of the new identity across every channel. Strategic input to align image, narrative, and digital experience.

The Solution

Defined the type of imagery and messaging able to carry a coherent brand. New visual and verbal identity, applied to social, web, editorial pieces, and email marketing. The tone becomes more playful, visually recognizable, and aligned to a defined personality.
